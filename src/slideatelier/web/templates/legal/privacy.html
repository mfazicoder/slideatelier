Privacy policy

Last updated: launch day, 2026

Coming soon — full policy in preparation.

slideAtelier is currently in soft launch. We are finalising the long-form privacy policy with counsel. In the interim, here is what you should know:

  • Your decks, brief content, and uploaded files are your IP. We do not train models on your content.
  • We use Anthropic Claude as the AI provider. Your prompts pass through Anthropic's API under their standard data-handling terms.
  • We store account email + hashed password (bcrypt), session tokens, and your deck artifacts. That's it.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent personal data to third parties.

For any privacy question, data deletion request, or concern, email founder@slideatelier.com directly.
