Head to head

Honest takes on each.

For every competitor: where they shine, where we think Hatchik wins, and an honest "pick them if…" line. If their pitch already sold you, pick them — that's fine.

Bolt.new

"Prompt, run, edit, and deploy full-stack web apps."

Where they shine

Unbeatable first thirty seconds. You describe an app, it appears in the browser, and you can click around. For sketching a concept, showing a co-founder, or pitching at a hackathon, very little touches it.

Where Hatchik wins

Bolt's output is a prototype. There's no real auth, no payments, no mailboxes on your domain, no mobile shells, and no clean way to take it to your own GitHub and keep building elsewhere. We start where Bolt stops — you get a wired, running stack your AI tool of choice continues to build on for years.

Pick Bolt if… you want to throw together a demo to show someone in five minutes. We're not built for that.

Pick Hatchik if… the "demo" is going to charge real money to real people, and you want it on your own domain, code, and server from day one.

Pro plan ~$20/mo24

Lovable.dev

"Build software products, using only a chat interface."

Where they shine

Best-in-class generated UI. Their AI has good taste, and the Supabase-backed apps it produces look polished out of the box. If you're starting from zero on design and you like working in their chat, Lovable feels effortless.

Where Hatchik wins

The chat is Lovable — if you're already paying for Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Cline, Codex, or Antigravity, you have to context-switch into a second AI you don't know. We don't ship our own AI; we plug your existing one into a real, pre-wired backend (auth, payments via Paddle MoR, mailboxes, mobile shells), with the code in your GitHub. No second relationship to manage.

Pick Lovable if… you don't already have an AI coding tool you love and you want one stop for AI + design + light backend.

Pick Hatchik if… you already drive Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Cline, Codex, or Antigravity daily, and you want them building on something real instead of starting over in someone else's chat.

From ~$25/mo (Pro) up to ~$100/mo (higher plans)25

Replit Agent

"Build and deploy apps from natural language in the Replit IDE."

Where they shine

Genuinely vertically integrated — the IDE, the AI, the runtime, deploy, secrets manager, and database all sit in one tab. For learners and for people who like living inside one browser environment, that coherence is a real advantage.

Where Hatchik wins

Replit's audience wants the IDE. Ours wants to live in Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Cline, Codex, or Antigravity on their own laptop, push to their own GitHub, and run on their own server. Pricing also escalates quickly with Agent usage, and the platform-managed services don't really come with you if you leave. With Hatchik you can hand the keys back any time and keep running the same stack independently for around £10–15/month.

Pick Replit if… you want everything in one tab, you're new to coding, and you're happy to grow inside their environment.

Pick Hatchik if… you want a real local-feeling workflow with your editor and AI, on infrastructure that's yours.

Replit Core $25/mo + Agent usage on top26

Bubble.io

"The best way to build digital products without code."

Where they shine

A genuine no-code platform with more than a decade of refinement, a huge community, and a workflow engine that can run real businesses. People do make money on Bubble, and if you're confident you'll never need to touch source code, it's a defensible choice.

Where Hatchik wins

Bubble is a closed visual runtime. There's no source code to take with you, your AI coding tool can't see inside it, and the day you outgrow Bubble's pricing or limits is the day you start rebuilding from scratch. Hatchik gives you a real codebase in your own GitHub from minute one — your AI can read and change anything, and the exit cost is zero.

Pick Bubble if… you genuinely never want to look at code or work with an AI coding tool, and you're happy to live inside their ecosystem long-term.

Pick Hatchik if… you're already comfortable letting AI write code, and you'd rather not bet the next five years on a single vendor's platform.

Starter ~$39/mo, Growth ~$119/mo27

ShipFast (and other boilerplates)

"The Next.js boilerplate to ship your startup in days, not weeks."

Where they shine

A genuinely thoughtful code drop. Marc Lou and the boilerplate scene have done a great job packaging auth, Stripe, email and SEO patterns into one well-documented Next.js repo. If you can read TypeScript, you can be productive on day one.

Where Hatchik wins

A boilerplate is code, not a running stack. You still need to register the domain, set up Stripe (and become merchant of record yourself for global tax), configure Resend or Postmark, provision a server, wire DNS, build the iOS and Android shells, and keep it all patched. Hatchik does all of that for you — with Paddle as MoR so VAT/GST/sales tax just works — while still giving you the same kind of clean code in your GitHub.

Pick ShipFast if… you're a confident engineer who enjoys setting up infrastructure and wants a one-off lifetime licence.

Pick Hatchik if… you'd rather skip the infrastructure setup entirely, especially the tax/payments complexity, and let an AI build on top of something already running.

~$199 one-off lifetime licence28

DIY on Vercel + Supabase

"Just wire it together yourself, it's a weekend."

Where they shine

If you're an engineer who already knows what you want, this stack is excellent. Vercel does edge hosting beautifully, Supabase gives you Postgres, auth and storage in one place, and the docs for stitching it all together are first-rate.

Where Hatchik wins

DIY is roughly two to four weekends of plumbing before you write the first feature: Stripe live-mode, webhooks, transactional email setup, custom domain DNS, Capacitor for mobile, error monitoring, backups, and then the fun discovery that Stripe doesn't handle international tax for you. We do all of that in minutes, with Paddle as merchant of record so the global tax piece is genuinely solved — not paperwork you've quietly deferred.

Pick DIY if… infrastructure is fun for you and you'd genuinely enjoy spending a few weekends getting it right.

Pick Hatchik if… you'd rather spend those weekends on the actual product, with your AI tool, and skip the plumbing entirely.

Vercel Pro $20/mo + Supabase Pro $25/mo ≈ $45/mo combined29

FAQ

Straight answers.

Why is Hatchik cheaper than Bubble, Lovable or Replit?

Three reasons. One, we don't ship our own AI — you bring Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Cline, Codex, or Antigravity, so we don't carry the bill or the GPU costs the AI builders do. Two, we run on Hetzner servers in Europe instead of US-hyperscaler edge networks, which is dramatically cheaper for the same uptime when you don't need single-digit-millisecond latency. Three, we genuinely think the right price for "your first SaaS, properly wired" is about a tenner a month, not forty. The trade-off is that we're newer and smaller; the upside is that the savings get passed on.

What if I outgrow Hatchik?

Take everything with you. Your GitHub repo is yours, your domain is yours, your server is rented to your account, your customers' data is yours. We hand over the keys; you can keep running the exact same stack independently for roughly £10–15/month total. No exit fee, no proprietary lock-in, no rebuild. If anything, we'd rather you graduate and tell a friend than stay on a tier that doesn't fit.

I'm already on Bubble / Bolt / Lovable / Replit. Should I switch?

Honestly, only if you're hitting one of three problems: (a) you've moved past the demo stage and you keep bumping into the platform's limits, (b) you're paying more than £30/month and getting limited value back, or (c) you want your AI coding tool to read and edit the actual code, which closed platforms can't expose. If your current platform is working and the price feels right, stay. Hatchik exists for the moment a non-engineer decides they want real infrastructure they own — not as a blanket replacement for every tool.

Is Hatchik really not an AI builder?

Correct — we are not. We're the production substrate your AI coder builds on. Bolt and Lovable ship their own AI chat; we ship a real running stack (auth, payments, mail, mobile, GitHub repo, sandbox in seconds) that your existing AI tool of choice continues to extend. Think of us as the wiring and Cursor / Windsurf / Claude Code / Cline / Codex / Antigravity as the electrician you've already hired. It's a deliberate split: AI choice is moving fast, infrastructure standards aren't.

Where does the comparison data on this page come from?

Public pricing pages and documentation from each competitor as of late 2025 / early 2026. Where we couldn't find a hard public source for a specific number, we've softened the claim ("typically", "from") rather than asserting it. If a competitor's pricing or features have changed since we wrote this, please let us know — we'll update the page.
