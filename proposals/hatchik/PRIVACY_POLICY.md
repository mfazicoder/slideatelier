## 3. What we collect, when, and why

### Visiting hatchik.com

When you visit our website we collect:

- **Standard server log data** (IP address, browser type, referrer, the
  pages you viewed, timestamp). Used to monitor service health and
  protect against abuse. Retained for 30 days then deleted.
- **Anonymous usage analytics** via Plausible Analytics (cookie-less,
  no personal identifiers, aggregated only). We use this to understand
  which pages are popular. Retained for 12 months.

We do **not** use third-party advertising trackers, cross-site
fingerprinting, or any cookies that require consent under UK / EU
cookie law. So we don't show you a cookie banner.

### Joining the waitlist

When you submit your email to our waitlist we collect:

- **Your email address.** We use this only to send you Hatchik updates
  (typically 1–2 emails per month) and to notify you when Hatchik
  launches. Retained until you unsubscribe.
- **The date and time you signed up.** For our records and to honour
  early-supporter benefits if we offer any.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your email with anyone. We do not
add you to any other lists. Unsubscribe link in every email; you can
also email `privacy@hatchik.com` to be removed instantly.

Our email service provider is {{EMAIL_SERVICE_PROVIDER}} ({{e.g.
Resend Inc., USA — operating under Standard Contractual Clauses for
UK/EU data transfers}}).

### Emailing us

When you email us we keep the email content for as long as needed to
answer your question, then delete after 24 months unless we have a
legitimate reason to keep it longer (e.g. you're a customer and we
need it for support context).

## 4. Legal basis (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

We process your data on these legal bases:
- **Legitimate interest** for server logs, security, and abuse
  protection
- **Consent** for waitlist signups (you opted in by submitting your
  email; you can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing)
- **Contract necessity** for any future paid-customer data (covered
  separately in the customer agreement)
- **Legal obligation** where applicable (e.g. tax records once we have
  revenue)

## 5. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you — email
  `privacy@hatchik.com`, we'll respond within 30 days
- **Correct** inaccurate data — same email
- **Delete** your data ("right to erasure") — same email; we'll delete
  unless we have a legal obligation to keep it
- **Object** to processing — same email
- **Data portability** — we'll send you a structured export
- **Withdraw consent** at any time — unsubscribe link in every email
- **Complain** to the ICO (UK) or your local supervisory authority
  (EU) if you think we've mishandled your data — though we'd prefer
  you tell us first so we can fix it

## 8. Security

We protect your data with:
- HTTPS for all traffic to and from hatchik.com (TLS 1.2+)
- Encrypted-at-rest storage with our hosting and email providers
- Limited access to the waitlist data (only Hatchik team members who need
  it to send updates)
- No data exports to third parties without your explicit consent

We're a small operation. If we have a data breach we'll tell you
within 72 hours per UK GDPR.

If you're under 16, please don't submit your email to our waitlist.
